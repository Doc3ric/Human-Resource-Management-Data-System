MODULE 1A — SYNCHRONIZED RENEWAL STATUS & SIGNAL ARCHITECTURE
Additive to Module 1. Solves cross-module renewal drift for casual and JO/contractual personnel. PRINCIPLE: detection is distributed (any module may notice and flag a renewal signal); the source of truth is single (only an authoritative renewal act commits is_renewed). No module may infer renewal as a side effect of unrelated activity (submitting an IPCR target, editing a record, filing leave, appearing in payroll must NEVER silently set is_renewed=true).
1A.1 Single source of truth: is_renewed plus renewal effectivity dates are owned by the appointment/contract record in the Recruitment/Appointments module (M4), where the legal act lives. Every other module READS this; none writes it directly.
1A.2 Signal table (detection layer): create tbl_renewal_signal (signal_id PK; personnel_id FK→tbl_employee_master; rating_period; source_module ENUM('PERFORMANCE','RECORDS','LEAVE','RECRUITMENT','PAYROLL','OTHER'); signal_type VARCHAR e.g. IPCR_TARGET_SUBMITTED, RECORD_EDITED, CONTRACT_UPLOADED, LEAVE_FILED, PAYROLL_ACTIVE; signal_strength ENUM('AUTHORITATIVE','CORROBORATING','WEAK'); created_by FK→tbl_user; created_at TIMESTAMP; notes TEXT). When a module notices activity implying an employee is active, it INSERTS a signal row — it does not mutate is_renewed.
1A.3 Signal strength rules: AUTHORITATIVE = an actual signed appointment/contract with effectivity dates was recorded (in practice emitted only by M4 Appointments, or by Records when uploading the signed renewal contract through the IDCC pipeline as APPT-33A or the casual/JO contract type). AUTHORITATIVE alone may set is_renewed=true (via the commit service in 1A.6). CORROBORATING/WEAK = IPCR target submitted, record edited, leave filed, payroll presence — these accumulate as evidence and drive prompts ONLY; they never set the flag.
1A.4 Per-module behavior: Performance Management — checking that an employee submitted their IPCR target writes a CORROBORATING signal; if that employee is is_renewed=false, show inline: "This employee is not tagged as renewed for this period. IPCR submission recorded as a pending signal. Resolve renewal status before finalizing this rating cycle." It does NOT assume renewal. Records — editing a record writes nothing to renewal; only uploading the signed renewal/appointment document emits the AUTHORITATIVE signal. Leave/Payroll/others — CORROBORATING signals only. (JO/COS remain blocked from leave accrual per the cross-cutting guardrail regardless of signals.)
1A.5 Synchronized Renewal Status Widget: build ONE shared embeddable widget that every module renders off the same is_renewed value and the same tbl_renewal_signal rows, so the notification is inherently synchronized (one truth, no copies to drift). Wherever an employee with is_renewed=false appears (performance tracker, records view, batch renewal, personnel inventory), the widget shows a consistent badge, e.g.: "Not renewed for [rating_period] · [N] activity signals detected ([signal types]) · No authoritative renewal on file" using var(--color-warning-bg). Resolving status in one place updates it everywhere because there is only one source.
1A.6 Single commit path, many launch points: provide ONE shared batch-renewal commit service with all guardrails (JO/COS legality checks, mandatory effectivity-date capture, AUTHORITATIVE-signal-or-explicit-authority requirement, audit logging). Modules MAY expose their own launch buttons and filters in their own context (e.g., Performance Management "Process renewals for those who completed their rating cycle"; Records its own entry point), but ALL launch points call the SAME commit service — never their own write logic. What differs per module is the filter and framing; what stays identical is the commit path, the guardrails, and the audit trail. This prevents multiple code paths setting is_renewed under differing definitions (a COA-disallowance/data-drift risk).
1A.7 Commit authority (RBAC-governed): by default ONLY PHRMO / the Appointments Unit may execute the renewal commit. Authority is delegable solely through the Role-Permission matrix via a dedicated permission bit (e.g., renewal_commit). A module's launch button is visible/enabled only to sessions holding renewal_commit; all other roles may view status, add CORROBORATING signals, and request renewal, but cannot commit. Every commit and every delegation change is written to tbl_audit_log (who committed, for whom, rating period, effectivity dates, source signal).
1A.8 Request-to-renew flow (for non-authorized modules): a module without renewal_commit may raise a "Request Renewal" action that notifies PHRMO/Appointments and attaches the accumulated signals as supporting evidence; PHRMO then commits (or rejects with a logged reason). This lets originating offices surface real active-but-unpapered cases without granting them commit authority.
1A.9 Compliance: renewal of casual/JO personnel is a formal HR act tied to an actual appointment/contract with effectivity dates; the system must never represent inferred activity as a renewal. Tooltips cite 2025 ORAOHRA (appointment/renewal as a formal act) on the commit control and DOLE-CSC-COA-DBM JC No. 1 s.2017 on JO/COS rows.

MODULE 1B — EMPLOYEE LIFECYCLE EXCLUSION & RECORDS RETENTION MATRIX
Additive to Module 1/1A (active-status gating) and Module 2/Module 9 Stage 8 (NAP retention). Two linked capabilities: (A) extend the live-report exclusion beyond "not renewed" to ALL inactive lifecycle states (separated, retired, terminated, end-of-term, and unrenewed casual/JO/contractual), and (B) a portable, configurable Records Retention & Disposal Matrix in the Records module for easy online reference of each record series' lifecycle.
1B.1 Employee lifecycle status: every employee record carries a lifecycle_status (e.g., ACTIVE, RESIGNED, RETIRED, TERMINATED/DISMISSED, END_OF_TERM, TRANSFERRED, DECEASED, AWOL_DROPPED, NOT_RENEWED for casual/JO/contractual), an effective date of the status change, and the legal/administrative basis. A record is "active for reporting" only when lifecycle_status == ACTIVE and (for casual/JO/contractual) is_renewed == true for the current period.
1B.2 Live-report exclusion (extends the is_renewed gate): exclude from ALL live statistical reports and any operational report — Executive Dashboard, GAD/analytics, Personnel Inventory, Performance Management/SPMS, and every Module 12 scoreboard — any employee who is not active-for-reporting: i.e., regular/permanent who are separated/retired/terminated, AND casual/JO/contractual who are not renewed. As with Module 1, this is query-level exclusion (WHERE lifecycle_status='ACTIVE' AND is_renewed=true where applicable) — records are NEVER deleted at this stage, only filtered out of live views so statistics reflect the genuinely active workforce. Historical/longitudinal reports may still include past personnel where explicitly requested and permitted, clearly labeled as historical.
1B.3 Archival on separation: when an employee becomes inactive (separation/retirement/termination/end-of-term, or casual/JO/contractual non-renewal that ends the engagement), their record is moved to an ARCHIVED state — retained, secured, removed from live/active rosters and reports, but fully preserved and retrievable by authorized Records staff. Archiving is a status transition, not deletion. 201 files are handled as Confidential/Restricted records per NAP Circular 4. The archived record continues to accrue its retention clock toward eventual disposition.
1B.4 Lawful disposal only (never auto-delete): records are disposed of (destroyed) only after their NAP-approved retention period lapses AND only through the audited disposal workflow — NEVER by an automated hard-delete. Per R.A. 9470 Sec. 18 Art. III, no public record may be disposed of or destroyed except with the prior written authority of the NAP Executive Director. The system computes when a record becomes ELIGIBLE for disposal (retention elapsed), routes it to the Disposal View (NAP Form No. 1 inventory layout, Module 2.3), and keeps the hard-delete locked until the signed NAP Form No. 3 (Authority to Dispose) is uploaded and verified. Permanent records (e.g., appointments CS Form 33-A/B, core 201 assets) are NEVER eligible for disposal — retained indefinitely. The system marks records as Temporary (with a retention period) or Permanent per the matrix in 1B.5.
1B.5 PORTABLE RECORDS RETENTION & DISPOSAL MATRIX (configurable online reference): build a Records-module setting/screen — a portable, user-friendly matrix that lets users look up, for any record series, its full lifecycle: how long it stays active in office, how long in storage/archive, and its final disposition (permanent preservation or destruction). Implement as a configurable table tbl_retention_schedule (series_id, record_series_title, description, record_category e.g. 201/Leave/Appointment/Disciplinary/Financial/LGU, time_value ENUM('PERMANENT','TEMPORARY'), active_period, storage_period, total_retention, disposition_action ENUM('PERMANENT_PRESERVATION','DESTRUCTION','REVIEW'), legal_basis, nap_grds_item_ref, remarks, last_updated). Seed it from the current NAP General Records Disposition Schedule (the 2023/2024 updated GRDS under NAP General Circular No. 5) and the agency's own Records Disposition Schedule (NAP Form No. 2), and make it editable by the Records Officer so it stays current as NAP issues updates. The matrix is the single online reference that drives the retention clock for every document/record (the IDCC Stage 8 retention_class maps to a series in this matrix — single source, no divergence). Display it as a searchable/filterable, exportable reference (search by series, category, disposition; export to PDF/CSV matching NAP Form No. 2 layout). Each employee/document view can show its applicable retention row inline ("This record series: 201 File — Confidential — retention per [legal basis] — disposition: [action] — eligible for disposal on [computed date]") so any user can see, at a glance, where a record is in its lifecycle and when it may be disposed.
1B.6 Lifecycle/retention dashboard (Records canonical scoreboard, per Module 12): the Records/IDCC scoreboard (canonical owner of document/retention stats) shows counts by lifecycle state (active/archived/eligible-for-disposal/pending NAP authority/disposed), records approaching retention end (advance warning so the Records Officer can prepare NAP Form No. 3), and disposal actions completed — aggregate only, RACCS items as anonymized counts, respecting RBAC. No new duplicate board; this extends the existing IDCC/Records owner row in the Metric Ownership Registry (12.8).
1B.7 RBAC & audit: lifecycle status changes (separation/archival) and any disposal action are gated to authorized Records/HRMO roles (lifecycle_manage, disposal_approve bits in Module 4A) and fully audit-logged. The retention matrix is editable only by the Records Officer role. Archived/separated personnel records, especially 201 and disciplinary, retain their confidentiality and RACCS gating after archival. Disposal eligibility is computed and SURFACED automatically, but execution always requires the human-authorized, NAP-cleared workflow.

Captures each employee's current leave balances and attendance status, detects violations related to leave administration (leave without pay, absence without pay, AWOL, undertime, tardiness, and related infractions), generates a dynamic watchlist/report of employees to be issued or already issued notices, generates the correct legally-grounded letter per violation type, auto-records any issued notice into the employee's record, and generates coordination letters to affected offices (e.g., Accounting/Payroll) for dropping from payroll or salary deduction. 2B.2 Violation detection thresholds (current law — make thresholds configurable in an admin table but seed with these defaults and cite them):
- HABITUAL TARDINESS (light offense, 2025 RACCS Rule 10 §63(C)(10)): tardiness, regardless of minutes, ten (10) times a month for at least two (2) months in a semester OR at least two (2) consecutive months during the year. Penalty ladder: reprimand (1st), 1–30 days suspension (2nd), dismissal (3rd).
- HABITUAL ABSENTEEISM (grave offense, 2025 RACCS): unauthorized absences exceeding the allowable 2.5 days monthly leave credit for at least three (3) months in a semester OR three (3) consecutive months in a year. Penalty: suspension 6 months 1 day to 1 year (1st), dismissal (2nd).
- AWOL (Omnibus Rules on Leave Sec. 63): continuously absent without approved leave for at least thirty (30) calendar days → separated/dropped from the rolls without prior notice; if less than 30 days, a written Return-to-Work Order is served to the last known address, and failure to return within the stated period is a valid ground to drop from the rolls. Also flag the circumvention pattern (substantial absences though less than 30 days, repeated such that a pattern is apparent).
- LWOP / ABSENCE WITHOUT PAY: flag when VL/SL credits are exhausted and further absence/undertime/tardiness converts to leave/absence without pay; track LWOP day totals for payroll coordination and for the Sec. 62 one-year-LWOP automatic-separation watch.
- UNDERTIME / TARDINESS DEDUCTION TRACKING: continuously deduct from VL; when VL is exhausted, subsequent undertime/tardiness becomes without pay and is flagged for payroll deduction.
The engine classifies each flagged employee by violation type and current offense count (1st/2nd/3rd) read from prior recorded notices, so the correct penalty tier and letter are selected.
