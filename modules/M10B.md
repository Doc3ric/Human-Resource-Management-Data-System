MODULE 10B — LEARNING & DEVELOPMENT (TRAINING) MODULE
Additive destination/HR module integrated with the IDCC/Records pipeline (M1), the EETE training-hours scoring in the deliberation workspace (Module 5.3 sub-section 3), and the GAD/Workforce analytics. Captures employee attendance to training and standard training details, generates Certificates of Attendance (individual or by batch) and a separate Certificate for resource speakers, supports certificate customization, and automatically links each completed training to the employee's record for future use in promotions and other HR actions. Grounded in CSC competency-based L&D and PRIME-HRM; training records feed the relevant-training-hours computation already used in recruitment scoring.
10B.1 Training detail capture (standard metadata for each training/activity): title; description/rationale; type (in-house, external, online, seminar, workshop, conference, scholarship, etc.); competency area / L&D category; conducting/sponsoring institution; venue (or platform); inclusive dates and times; total training hours/credit hours (this value flows to the EETE relevant-training-hours computation); organizer/coordinator; fund source; reference/authority (e.g., office order, approved L&D plan, Training Need Analysis link); and supporting documents (program, approved request, post-activity report) ingested through the IDCC pipeline.
10B.2 Attendance capture: register participants per training — link each to personnel_id where they are PGB personnel (so the record attaches to their 201/record), and allow non-PGB/external participants as ad-hoc entries (name, office/agency, designation) for certificate generation without a personnel link. Support multiple capture modes: manual entry, batch import (spreadsheet roster), and per-session check-in/out for multi-day activities (capturing actual attendance per session so partial attendance is visible). Record attendance status (Present/Partial/Absent/Excused) per participant per session; compute total hours actually attended vs total program hours. Flag partial attendance — certificates of completion/attendance should reflect actual hours where the program requires it.
10B.3 Certificate of Attendance generation (individual or batch): generate certificates for participants who meet the attendance threshold. Support (a) INDIVIDUAL generation for a single participant and (b) BATCH generation for the full qualifying roster in one operation (one PDF set or merged file). Each certificate auto-populates: participant full name (proper case), training title, inclusive dates, total hours, venue, conducting institution, and signatories. Certificates carry the PGB/PHRMO letterhead and the configured signatory hierarchy. Batch generation must handle the full roster efficiently and skip/flag participants below the attendance threshold with a clear list of who was excluded and why.
10B.4 Resource Speaker / Resource Person certificate: a SEPARATE certificate template for resource speakers, facilitators, trainers, and panelists — distinct wording ("in recognition of/appreciation for serving as Resource Speaker on [topic]…"), capturing the speaker's name, the topic/session delivered, date, and the engaging office. Generated individually or for a panel of speakers in batch. Resource-speaker engagements may also be recorded to the speaker's own employee record where they are PGB personnel (as a developmental/contribution credit usable in HR actions).
10B.5 Certificate customization: allow authorized users to customize certificate templates without code — configurable elements: title/heading text, body wording, logo(s)/seal, background/border design, fonts and colors (from theme-aligned options), signatory blocks (name, position, multiple signatories with order), date format, and an optional control/serial number and QR/verification code. Save multiple named templates (e.g., "Standard Attendance", "Completion with Hours", "Resource Speaker", "Webinar") and select per training. Provide a live preview before generation. Customization is template-level (reusable), not per-certificate hand-editing, to keep issuance consistent and auditable; per-certificate fields (name, hours) populate from data.
10B.6 Auto-link to employee record (for HR actions): on issuance, each PGB participant's certificate and the training record are automatically written to their employee record via the IDCC/Records pipeline (tbl_doc_metadata, doc_type e.g. TRAINING-CERT / TRAINING-RECORD), linked to personnel_id, capturing training title, hours, dates, competency area, and certificate reference. This builds a cumulative training history that: (a) feeds the relevant-training-hours figure in recruitment/promotion EETE scoring (Module 5.3 #3 and 6.2 training engine); (b) is retrievable for promotion applications, QS/competency validation, and other HR actions; and (c) is retained per NAP/RDS rules (Module 9 Stage 8). The training-hours value is single-sourced — the same record that issues the certificate is the record scoring reads, so there is no divergence between "certificate issued" and "hours credited."
10B.7 Training records & history view: a per-employee training history (chronological list with title, hours, dates, competency area, certificate link) accessible from their record, and a master training registry (all activities with rosters and issuance status). Searchable/filterable by competency area, date, employee, office. This view is what HRMO and the employee reference during promotion screening.
10B.8 L&D scoreboard (per Module 12, equitable/non-redundant): the canonical owner of training statistics — total trainings conducted/period, total participants, total training hours delivered, participation by office and by sex (GAD-disaggregated, reusing GAD demographics as reference tiles not re-counts), competency-area coverage, certificates issued, and average training hours per employee. FULL board tier. Respects RBAC and the is_renewed/active gate; aggregate only. Other modules referencing training hours (e.g., recruitment) show a reference tile linking here, not a duplicate board. Register "training statistics" to L&D in the Metric Ownership Registry (12.8).
10B.9 RBAC: a Learning & Development section in the RBAC matrix (Module 4A) with bits for training_create, attendance_manage, certificate_generate, template_customize, and training_record_view. Certificate issuance and template customization are gated to authorized L&D/HRMO staff; employees may view their own training history. All issuance and template changes are audit-logged.
10B.10 Compliance & citations: align with CSC competency-based L&D, PRIME-HRM, and the agency's approved L&D plan / Training Need Analysis; training hours support QS and promotion under 2025 ORAOHRA and DBM-CSC JC No. 1 s.2017; personal data on certificates and rosters handled under R.A. 10173. Tooltips cite these on the training detail and certificate controls.

CROSS-CUTTING JO/COS GUARDRAIL: employment_category ENUM('PERMANENT','CASUAL','COTERMINOUS','JO','COS'); per DOLE-CSC-COA-DBM JC No. 1 s.2017, block JO/COS from leave accrual, minimum-QS appointment validation, and RACCS matrices.
