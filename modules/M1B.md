MODULE 1B — EMPLOYEE LIFECYCLE EXCLUSION & RECORDS RETENTION MATRIX
Additive to Module 1/1A (active-status gating) and Module 2/Module 9 Stage 8 (NAP retention). Two linked capabilities: (A) extend the live-report exclusion beyond "not renewed" to ALL inactive lifecycle states (separated, retired, terminated, end-of-term, and unrenewed casual/JO/contractual), and (B) a portable, configurable Records Retention & Disposal Matrix in the Records module for easy online reference of each record series' lifecycle.
1B.1 Employee lifecycle status: every employee record carries a lifecycle_status (e.g., ACTIVE, RESIGNED, RETIRED, TERMINATED/DISMISSED, END_OF_TERM, TRANSFERRED, DECEASED, AWOL_DROPPED, NOT_RENEWED for casual/JO/contractual), an effective date of the status change, and the legal/administrative basis. A record is "active for reporting" only when lifecycle_status == ACTIVE and (for casual/JO/contractual) is_renewed == true for the current period.
1B.2 Live-report exclusion (extends the is_renewed gate): exclude from ALL live statistical reports and any operational report — Executive Dashboard, GAD/analytics, Personnel Inventory, Performance Management/SPMS, and every Module 12 scoreboard — any employee who is not active-for-reporting: i.e., regular/permanent who are separated/retired/terminated, AND casual/JO/contractual who are not renewed. As with Module 1, this is query-level exclusion (WHERE lifecycle_status='ACTIVE' AND is_renewed=true where applicable) — records are NEVER deleted at this stage, only filtered out of live views so statistics reflect the genuinely active workforce. Historical/longitudinal reports may still include past personnel where explicitly requested and permitted, clearly labeled as historical.
1B.3 Archival on separation: when an employee becomes inactive (separation/retirement/termination/end-of-term, or casual/JO/contractual non-renewal that ends the engagement), their record is moved to an ARCHIVED state — retained, secured, removed from live/active rosters and reports, but fully preserved and retrievable by authorized Records staff. Archiving is a status transition, not deletion. 201 files are handled as Confidential/Restricted records per NAP Circular 4. The archived record continues to accrue its retention clock toward eventual disposition.
1B.4 Lawful disposal only (never auto-delete): records are disposed of (destroyed) only after their NAP-approved retention period lapses AND only through the audited disposal workflow — NEVER by an automated hard-delete. Per R.A. 9470 Sec. 18 Art. III, no public record may be disposed of or destroyed except with the prior written authority of the NAP Executive Director. The system computes when a record becomes ELIGIBLE for disposal (retention elapsed), routes it to the Disposal View (NAP Form No. 1 inventory layout, Module 2.3), and keeps the hard-delete locked until the signed NAP Form No. 3 (Authority to Dispose) is uploaded and verified. Permanent records (e.g., appointments CS Form 33-A/B, core 201 assets) are NEVER eligible for disposal — retained indefinitely. The system marks records as Temporary (with a retention period) or Permanent per the matrix in 1B.5.
1B.5 PORTABLE RECORDS RETENTION & DISPOSAL MATRIX (configurable online reference): build a Records-module setting/screen — a portable, user-friendly matrix that lets users look up, for any record series, its full lifecycle: how long it stays active in office, how long in storage/archive, and its final disposition (permanent preservation or destruction). Implement as a configurable table tbl_retention_schedule (series_id, record_series_title, description, record_category e.g. 201/Leave/Appointment/Disciplinary/Financial/LGU, time_value ENUM('PERMANENT','TEMPORARY'), active_period, storage_period, total_retention, disposition_action ENUM('PERMANENT_PRESERVATION','DESTRUCTION','REVIEW'), legal_basis, nap_grds_item_ref, remarks, last_updated). Seed it from the current NAP General Records Disposition Schedule (the 2023/2024 updated GRDS under NAP General Circular No. 5) and the agency's own Records Disposition Schedule (NAP Form No. 2), and make it editable by the Records Officer so it stays current as NAP issues updates. The matrix is the single online reference that drives the retention clock for every document/record (the IDCC Stage 8 retention_class maps to a series in this matrix — single source, no divergence). Display it as a searchable/filterable, exportable reference (search by series, category, disposition; export to PDF/CSV matching NAP Form No. 2 layout). Each employee/document view can show its applicable retention row inline ("This record series: 201 File — Confidential — retention per [legal basis] — disposition: [action] — eligible for disposal on [computed date]") so any user can see, at a glance, where a record is in its lifecycle and when it may be disposed.
1B.6 Lifecycle/retention dashboard (Records canonical scoreboard, per Module 12): the Records/IDCC scoreboard (canonical owner of document/retention stats) shows counts by lifecycle state (active/archived/eligible-for-disposal/pending NAP authority/disposed), records approaching retention end (advance warning so the Records Officer can prepare NAP Form No. 3), and disposal actions completed — aggregate only, RACCS items as anonymized counts, respecting RBAC. No new duplicate board; this extends the existing IDCC/Records owner row in the Metric Ownership Registry (12.8).
1B.7 RBAC & audit: lifecycle status changes (separation/archival) and any disposal action are gated to authorized Records/HRMO roles (lifecycle_manage, disposal_approve bits in Module 4A) and fully audit-logged. The retention matrix is editable only by the Records Officer role. Archived/separated personnel records, especially 201 and disciplinary, retain their confidentiality and RACCS gating after archival. Disposal eligibility is computed and SURFACED automatically, but execution always requires the human-authorized, NAP-cleared workflow.
1B.8 Compliance citations (embed in the matrix, disposal view, and tooltips): R.A. 9470 (NAP Act of 2007) Sec. 18 Art. III (no disposal without NAP Executive Director authority); NAP General Records Disposition Schedule (current 2023/2024 GRDS under NAP General Circular No. 5); NAP Form No. 1 (Records Inventory), NAP Form No. 2 (Records Disposition Schedule), NAP Form No. 3 (Authority to Dispose); NAP Circular 4 (201 files = Confidential/Restricted); R.A. 10173 (privacy of separated-employee personal data — retained securely, disposed lawfully).
