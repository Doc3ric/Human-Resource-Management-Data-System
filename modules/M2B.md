MODULE 2B — LEAVE VIOLATION MONITORING, NOTICE GENERATION & PAYROLL COORDINATION
Additive to the Leave module (M2) and the IDCC/Records pipeline. Captures each employee's current leave balances and attendance status, detects violations related to leave administration (leave without pay, absence without pay, AWOL, undertime, tardiness, and related infractions), generates a dynamic watchlist/report of employees to be issued or already issued notices, generates the correct legally-grounded letter per violation type, auto-records any issued notice into the employee's record, and generates coordination letters to affected offices (e.g., Accounting/Payroll) for dropping from payroll or salary deduction. JO/COS are excluded from civil-service leave workflows per the cross-cutting guardrail and are not subject to leave-credit violations (their engagements are governed by their contracts, not the Omnibus Rules on Leave).
2B.1 Data capture: read current VL/SL balances from tbl_leave_ledger; read attendance/DTR data (tardiness minutes, undertime, absences, LWOP days) for the rating period. Compute per employee: VL balance, SL balance, total tardiness instances per month, total undertime, count of unauthorized/LWOP absence days, and consecutive-absence streak. Tardiness and undertime are deducted from VACATION leave credits only, never sick leave, unless the undertime is for health reasons supported by a medical certificate and leave application (Omnibus Rules on Leave, CSC MC No. 41 s.1998, Sec. 34). Offsetting tardiness/absences by working beyond regular hours is NOT allowed (per CSC enhanced work-hours guidelines); the engine must not net these out.
2B.2 Violation detection thresholds (current law — make thresholds configurable in an admin table but seed with these defaults and cite them):
- HABITUAL TARDINESS (light offense, 2025 RACCS Rule 10 §63(C)(10)): tardiness, regardless of minutes, ten (10) times a month for at least two (2) months in a semester OR at least two (2) consecutive months during the year. Penalty ladder: reprimand (1st), 1–30 days suspension (2nd), dismissal (3rd).
- HABITUAL ABSENTEEISM (grave offense, 2025 RACCS): unauthorized absences exceeding the allowable 2.5 days monthly leave credit for at least three (3) months in a semester OR three (3) consecutive months in a year. Penalty: suspension 6 months 1 day to 1 year (1st), dismissal (2nd).
- AWOL (Omnibus Rules on Leave Sec. 63): continuously absent without approved leave for at least thirty (30) calendar days → separated/dropped from the rolls without prior notice; if less than 30 days, a written Return-to-Work Order is served to the last known address, and failure to return within the stated period is a valid ground to drop from the rolls. Also flag the circumvention pattern (substantial absences though less than 30 days, repeated such that a pattern is apparent).
- LWOP / ABSENCE WITHOUT PAY: flag when VL/SL credits are exhausted and further absence/undertime/tardiness converts to leave/absence without pay; track LWOP day totals for payroll coordination and for the Sec. 62 one-year-LWOP automatic-separation watch.
- UNDERTIME / TARDINESS DEDUCTION TRACKING: continuously deduct from VL; when VL is exhausted, subsequent undertime/tardiness becomes without pay and is flagged for payroll deduction.
The engine classifies each flagged employee by violation type and current offense count (1st/2nd/3rd) read from prior recorded notices, so the correct penalty tier and letter are selected.
2B.3 Dynamic violation report/watchlist: a live, filterable report listing employees with detected or pending violations, grouped by violation type and by office/unit, showing: name, office, current VL/SL balance, the specific violation, the counts driving it (e.g., tardiness instances per month across the qualifying months), offense number, current status (Detected / Notice Pending / Notice Issued / Responded / Resolved / Escalated), and the recommended action. Filters: by office, violation type, offense tier, rating period, status. Sort by severity. Export to CSV/PDF with the standard header/footer and legal citations. This report is the issuance worklist for HRMO.
2B.4 Letter generation (per violation, current law): generate the correct letter template based on violation type and offense tier. Distinct templates at minimum: (a) Return-to-Work Order (AWOL < 30 days, Omnibus Rules Sec. 63); (b) Notice of Habitual Tardiness with the applicable penalty tier (2025 RACCS Rule 10 §63(C)(10)); (c) Notice of Habitual Absenteeism (grave offense, 2025 RACCS); (d) Notice/advice on LWOP and its consequences including the Sec. 62 one-year automatic-separation rule; (e) Show-Cause / notice to explain where due process requires the employee to respond before penalty. Each letter must: cite the specific current legal basis and section; state the documented facts (dates, counts, balances) pulled from the data; observe due-process language (opportunity to explain where required before any penalty is imposed); include signature blocks per the office's signature hierarchy; and carry the office letterhead. Letters are drafts for HRMO review and signature — the system generates and routes them, it does not by itself impose penalties. Penalties for grave offenses require the formal administrative process under 2025 RACCS; the system's letters initiate/serve notice, not adjudicate.
2B.5 Auto-recording into the employee record: when a notice/letter is officially issued (marked Issued by an authorized user), the system writes the issuance into the employee's record through the IDCC/Records pipeline — creating a tbl_doc_metadata entry (doc_type e.g. LEAVE-VIOL-NOTICE), linking it to the personnel_id, attaching the generated letter PDF, recording issuance date, violation type, offense tier, and issuing officer, and writing an audit-log row. This builds the documented offense history that drives future offense-tier counting. Records of disciplinary notices follow retention rules (permanent if resulting in major penalty/dismissal, otherwise long-term cap) per Module 9 Stage 8.
2B.6 Office/payroll coordination letters: generate coordination letters to the office that needs to act or be informed. At minimum: (a) a letter to the Accounting/Payroll Office to DROP the employee from the payroll (for AWOL/dropped-from-rolls/separation cases) citing the legal basis and effective date; (b) a letter to Accounting/Payroll FOR INFORMATION on salary deduction (for LWOP days, undertime/tardiness that became without pay after VL exhaustion) with the computed days/amounts to deduct; (c) a letter informing the employee's home office/Department Head of the action. Each coordination letter pulls the computed figures (LWOP days, deduction basis, effectivity), carries proper letterhead and signature hierarchy, and on official issuance is recorded via IDCC and audit-logged. Do not transmit payroll actions silently — they are formal letters routed for signature, then recorded.
2B.7 Due process & safeguards: the system must support the civil-service due-process sequence — for offenses requiring it, a show-cause/notice-to-explain precedes any penalty recommendation; the report status flow (Detected → Notice Pending → Issued → Responded → Resolved/Escalated) enforces that an employee response window is captured before escalation. The system flags but does not auto-impose grave-offense penalties; those route to the formal 2025 RACCS administrative process (and, where confidential, into the RACCS wall, Module 9 Stage 5 / Module 10 M3). All thresholds, penalty tiers, and letter templates are admin-configurable so they can be updated when CSC issuances change, but ship seeded with the current law cited in 2B.2.
2B.8 RBAC: visible under a Leave Violation sub-module in the RBAC matrix (Module 4A). Detection/report viewing for HRMO Leave Administration staff; letter generation and issuance gated to authorized HRMO officers; payroll coordination issuance gated appropriately; only authorized roles may mark a notice Issued (which triggers auto-recording). Every detection-to-issuance action is audit-logged.
2B.9 Compliance citations (embed in report footers, letters, and tooltips): Omnibus Rules on Leave (CSC MC No. 41 s.1998) Sec. 34 (tardiness/undertime vs VL), Sec. 62 (one-year LWOP automatic separation), Sec. 63 (AWOL/Return-to-Work Order/30-day rule); 2025 RACCS Rule 10 §63 (habitual tardiness light offense; habitual absenteeism grave offense; penalty ladders); CSC enhanced government work-hours guidelines (no offsetting); R.A. 6713 (Code of Conduct). DOLE-CSC-COA-DBM JC No. 1 s.2017 note on JO/COS exclusion from these workflows.
