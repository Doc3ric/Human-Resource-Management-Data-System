2B.10 LEAVE-FILING PROCEDURE VIOLATIONS (late/improper filing): in addition to attendance violations, the engine detects violations of the leave APPLICATION/FILING rules and generates the corresponding letter with current citations. Detect and classify, per the Omnibus Rules on Leave (CSC MC No. 41 s.1998) and current CSC issuances:
- LATE FILING OF SICK LEAVE (Sec. 53): sick-leave application not filed immediately upon the employee's return from leave; flag when filing date exceeds the prescribed/return window. Sick leave in excess of five (5) successive days lacking the required medical certificate is a separate deficiency flag; where medical consultation was not availed, an affidavit is required in lieu — flag if neither is attached.
- LATE/IMPROPER FILING OF VACATION LEAVE (Sec. 51): VL not submitted five (5) days in advance of the effective date (whenever possible); flag advance-notice violations distinguishing genuinely unforeseeable cases.
- LATE FILING OF SPECIAL PRIVILEGE LEAVE / SLB / other leave types: filed outside the prescribed prior-filing window (e.g., SPL at least one week prior except emergencies); flag per the specific leave type's rule.
- MISSING/DEFICIENT SUPPORTING DOCUMENTS: required medical certificate, proof, or affidavit not attached for the leave type/duration that requires it.
- UNFILED ABSENCE / FAILURE TO FILE: absence incurred with no leave application filed at all within the prescribed period.
Consequence logic: failure to file within the prescribed period may cause DISAPPROVAL of the application (current CSC rule). A disapproved or unfiled leave converts the absence to UNAUTHORIZED — without pay, and counting toward habitual absenteeism / AWOL tracking in 2B.2. The engine links a filing violation to its downstream effect (disapproval recommendation, conversion to LWOP, escalation to attendance-violation tracking) so the two are consistent and not double-counted.
2B.11 FILING-VIOLATION LETTERS & ACTIONS: generate the correct letter per filing violation, each citing the specific current section: (a) Notice of Late/Improper Filing of Leave (citing Sec. 53 for sick leave, Sec. 51 for vacation leave, or the relevant leave-type rule) stating the facts (leave type, dates, filing date vs prescribed period) and the consequence; (b) Notice of Disapproval of Leave Application with the legal ground and the resulting status (absence rendered unauthorized/without pay) and the employee's remedy/period to respond; (c) Notice to Submit Lacking Requirement (medical certificate/affidavit) with the deadline; (d) where a pattern of late/improper filing forms part of habitual absenteeism or warrants disciplinary action, route to the show-cause/notice-to-explain flow (2B.4) and, for grave matters, to the formal 2025 RACCS process. Letters observe due process (opportunity to explain before adverse action where required), carry letterhead and signature hierarchy, and on official issuance auto-record to the employee's record via IDCC and audit-log (2B.5). These letters and their statuses flow through the same Detected→Notice Pending→Issued→Responded→Resolved/Escalated lifecycle and the same Leave-Violation RBAC and scoreboard as attendance violations — filing violations appear as their own category in the watchlist and the 2B scoreboard, without duplicating the attendance counts. Thresholds, windows, and templates are admin-configurable, seeded with the current law. Add to 2B.9 citations: Omnibus Rules on Leave Sec. 51 (vacation leave filing 5 days in advance), Sec. 53 (sick leave filing immediately upon return; medical certificate beyond 5 days), Sec. 54/56, and the current CSC rule that failure to file within the prescribed period may cause disapproval; CSC MC No. 41 s.1998 as amended (incl. CSC MC No. 05 s.2021 / CS Form No. 6 revised).
