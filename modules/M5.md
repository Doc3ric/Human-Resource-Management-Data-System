MODULE 5 — SPLIT-SCREEN DELIBERATION WORKSPACE & SCREENING
5.1 Entry & fixed header: per-row "Open Deliberation Panel" (clipboard icon) in the existing Active Vacancies grid; routes position ID + vacancy context to a locked non-scrollable header bar: Position Title, Item Number, Salary Grade, Target Office, phase badge [Screening]/[TWG Evaluation]/[HRMPSB Deliberation]/[Completed].
5.2 Split-screen: CSS Grid; Left Panel (Applicant Profile) 45%, Right Panel (TWG Scoring) 55%; independent overflow-y auto; height = viewport minus header; below 1024px collapse to one column with a "Profile"/"Scoring Sheet" tab switcher. Full-width applicant selector track spans both panels: clickable name chips reload both panels; active chip var(--color-accent); 24px circular photo thumbnail; missing photo → silhouette with var(--color-danger). All colors from theme.css.
5.3 Left panel read-only profile:
  PHOTO BLOCK 120px circular, 3px var(--color-accent) border. Hierarchy: manual upload first; if null query the 201-file module and auto-bind any found photo; if both null show red "Photo Required" + "Upload Photo Now" (JPG/PNG/WEBP <5MB) and block right-panel submit with "Scoring cannot be submitted until a photo is attached to this applicant's profile. Upload a photo to proceed."
  IDENTITY: full name 18px bold var(--color-primary); "Age: [N] years old"; vacancy label var(--color-text-muted).
  CURRENT EMPLOYMENT: employer, designation, status badge (Permanent/Casual/Contractual/Job Order/Contract of Service/Private Sector/Unemployed); government personnel also show agency + Salary Grade.
  QUALIFICATIONS — EIGHT accordions, default-open; each header has a document icon (var(--color-accent) opening the JAF/Google Drive link if present, else var(--color-border) disabled with tooltip "No supporting document attached"):
   1 Education: attainment badge (Elementary…Doctorate), course, university, year; green [Relevant] / red [Below Minimum QS]. Source JAF.
   2 Civil Service Eligibility: title (e.g., Career Service Professional, RA 1080 - Physician), rating, conferment date, location; green [Meets Requirement] / red [Does Not Meet Requirement] / gray [Not Required]. Source JAF Google Drive.
   3 Relevant Training: aggregate relevant hours as a large bold integer (feeds scoring); list name, hours, institution, completion date; relevant first. Source JAF.
   4 Awards: total count; list title, conferring authority, year; geographic badge (International…Agency/Office).
   5 IPCR: latest numeric + adjectival badge (Outstanding/VS var(--color-success); Satisfactory var(--color-warning); Unsat/Poor var(--color-danger)); period + office; "View History" toggle.
   6 Length of Service: total government tenure (years/months); chronological by agency, designation, dates; private sector isolated under "Private Sector" with note "For reference only. Does not count toward government service length for scoring purposes unless the position's rating criteria explicitly includes it."
   7 Demerit Record: green [No Demerit on Record] or red [Demerit on Record — N Item(s)] with case nature, decision date, penalty, status (Served/Pending/Appealed); footer "SECURITY NOTICE: Demerit information is governed by R.A. 10173. Access is restricted to authorized HRMPSB members only. All views are read-only and logged dynamically to the system audit trail."
   8 Relevant Experience (EETE): total position-relevant years/months; chronological rows (inclusive dates, position, agency+office/division, bulleted duties); auto-tag green [Relevant Experience] per DBM-CSC JC No. 1 s.2017 else gray [General Experience Only]; EETE pulls from JAF; active icon launches secure Google Drive link.
  FOOTER: "Profile data sourced from applicant's submitted documents and 201-file. Last updated: [date]. For corrections, contact HR Records Section."
5.4 Examination routing engine: report dashboard filters a trailing 9-month default window. Classify: (1) PGB JO (status=='Job Order' AND employer=='PGB'); (2) External (employer!='PGB' OR is_external==true); (3) Exempted Special Positions under 2025 ORAOHRA (licensed Medical Doctors, Legal Officers, specialized IT, or board-exempted). If is_exam_exempt==true, exclude from clustering and public posting, tag "Exempt from Examination — Advances to Technical/Board Assessment". Cluster non-exempt into tables of max 6 (default 6, adjustable); Date/Time/Room examiner-supplied before generation.
